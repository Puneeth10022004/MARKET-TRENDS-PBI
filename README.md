# MARKET-TRENDS-PBI
USA Market Shopping Trends Dashboard

1. Project Title / Headline

📊 USA Market Shopping Trends – Power BI Dashboard

An interactive and visually engaging Power BI dashboard designed to analyze shopping behavior, customer trends, revenue performance, and payment preferences across the USA retail market.

2. Short Description / Purpose

This dashboard provides detailed insights into customer purchasing patterns, seasonal sales impact, product category performance, and market trends. It helps businesses and analysts monitor revenue, customer ratings, payment methods, and demographic-based shopping behavior through interactive visualizations.

The dashboard is built to support data-driven decision-making for retail businesses, marketing teams, and business analysts.

3. Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

📊 Power BI Desktop – Main data visualization and reporting platform
📂 Power Query – Data cleaning, transformation, and preprocessing
🧠 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Used for calculated measures and KPIs
🔗 Data Modeling – Relationships created between multiple retail datasets
📁 File Format – .pbix for dashboard development and .png for dashboard previews
4. Data Source
Source:

Retail shopping and customer behavior dataset containing:

Customer demographics
Revenue and profit metrics
Product categories
Payment methods
Seasonal shopping trends
Customer ratings and spending behavior

The dataset is structured to simulate USA retail market trends and customer purchasing analytics.

5. Features / Highlights
📌 Business Problem

Retail businesses often struggle to identify customer buying patterns, top-performing product categories, and seasonal impacts on sales. Manual analysis makes it difficult to extract actionable insights quickly.

🎯 Goal of the Dashboard

The goal of this dashboard is to:

Track overall sales and revenue performance
Analyze customer spending habits by age and gender
Monitor seasonal shopping trends
Identify top profit-generating categories
Understand payment method preferences
Provide interactive filtering for deeper analysis
📈 Dashboard Highlights
✅ Sales Analysis Page
KPI cards for Total Sales, Profit, Discounts, and Categories
Gender-based sales distribution
Customer loyalty and spending level analysis
Product size-wise sales comparison
Geographic sales visualization using maps
Category and color performance analysis
✅ Market Trends Page
Revenue growth tracking
Customer rating analysis
Seasonal impact on shopping trends
Payment method usage breakdown
Customer spending by age group
Dynamic slicers for Season, Age, Payment Method, and Category
✅ Interactive Features
Cross-filtering visuals
Dynamic slicers and drill-down analysis
Responsive dashboard layout
Easy navigation across report pages
6. Key Insights
Clothing category generated the highest profit among all categories
Customers aged 50+ showed higher spending behavior
Credit cards and PayPal were among the most used payment methods
Seasonal trends significantly influenced customer purchases
Male customers contributed a larger share of total sales in the dataset
7. Conclusion

This Power BI dashboard transforms raw retail shopping data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations and analytical reporting. It enables users to explore market behavior, customer preferences, and sales performance efficiently, helping support smarter business decisions.
